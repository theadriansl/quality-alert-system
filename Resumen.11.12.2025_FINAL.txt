================================================================================
REPORTE DE ANALISIS DE ARQUITECTURA - QUALITY ALERT SYSTEM
Fecha: 12 de Noviembre de 2025
================================================================================

RESUMEN EJECUTIVO
================================================================================

Se realizo un analisis completo de la arquitectura del Quality Alert System,
identificando la estructura de base de datos PostgreSQL, endpoints del backend
en Node.js/Express, servicios del frontend en React, y las paginas principales
de la aplicacion. Se encontraron multiples incoherencias entre las capas de la
aplicacion que requieren atencion inmediata.

================================================================================
1. ESTRUCTURA DE BASE DE DATOS POSTGRESQL
================================================================================

Total de Tablas Implementadas: 10 tablas

1.1 MODULO DE USUARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- users
  Campos: id, email, password, first_name, last_name, role, department, phone,
          is_tft_member, permissions, created_at, updated_at
  Indices: email, is_tft_member

1.2 MODULO DE CLIENTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- clients
  Campos: id, name, alias, vendor_number, corporate_address, corporate_phone,
          corporate_fax, billing_address, billing_frequency, billing_period,
          website, is_active, requires_signature, created_at, updated_at
  Indices: name, alias, is_active

- client_contacts
  Campos: id, client_id, name, title, email, phone, created_at, updated_at
  Relacion: FK a clients(id) CASCADE

- client_documents
  Campos: id, client_id, file_name, title, file_size, file_type, file_path,
          server_file_name, uploaded_by, description, uploaded_at
  Relacion: FK a clients(id) CASCADE

- client_timeline
  Campos: id, client_id, event_type, event_category, description, details (JSONB),
          user_name, created_at
  Indices: client_id, event_category, created_at DESC
  Relacion: FK a clients(id) CASCADE

1.3 MODULO DE PROYECTOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- projects
  Campos: id, project_number, project_name, client_id, client_name, description,
          status, start_date, target_end_date, created_at, updated_at
  Indices: project_number, client_id, status
  Relacion: FK a clients(id) CASCADE

- project_parts
  Campos: id, project_id, part_number, client_part_number, part_name, description,
          revision, specifications, weight, snp_quantity, snp_volume, unit_cost,
          currency, created_at, updated_at
  Indices: project_id, part_number
  Relacion: FK a projects(id) CASCADE

1.4 MODULO 8D (8D Problem Solving)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- eightd_reports
  Campos principales: id, report_id, title, description, supplier_name,
          supplier_account, part_number, part_name, problem_type, severity,
          tipo_issue, tipo_resp, timing_occurrence, estimated_cost, issue_date,
          target_closure_date, customer_impact, status, current_step,
          progress_percentage
  Campos D1-D8: d1_team_members (JSONB), d1_champion_id, d2_problem_description,
          d3_containment_actions (JSONB), d4_root_causes (JSONB),
          d5_corrective_actions (JSONB), etc.
  Campos de asignacion: issue_assigned_to, countermeasure_assigned_to,
          confirmation_assigned_to
  Campo process_flow (JSONB)
  Indices: report_id, status, severity, current_step, issue_date DESC

- eightd_parts
  Campos: id, report_id, client_id, client_name, project_id, project_number,
          project_name, part_id, part_number, part_name, client_part_number,
          revision, description, unit_cost, currency, specifications (JSONB),
          created_at
  Indices: report_id, part_number
  Relacion: FK a eightd_reports(id) CASCADE

- eightd_status_history
  Campos: id, report_id, previous_status, new_status, previous_step, new_step,
          changed_by, change_reason, created_at
  Indices: report_id, created_at DESC
  Relacion: FK a eightd_reports(id) CASCADE

1.5 MODULO DE TEAM PRESETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- team_presets
  Campos: id, user_id, preset_name, issue_user_ids (JSONB),
          countermeasure_user_ids (JSONB), confirmation_user_ids (JSONB),
          created_at, updated_at
  Constraint: UNIQUE (user_id, preset_name)
  Relacion: FK a users(id) CASCADE
