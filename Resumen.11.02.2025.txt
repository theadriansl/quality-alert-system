   1.7  - Multiple labels identification
   1.8  - Gauging calibration & control
   1.9  - Product control after inspection
   1.10 - Inspection area proximity to customer
   1.11 - Rework in inspection area
   1.12 - Standardized Work Instructions
   1.13 - Lighting in inspection area
   1.14 - Health, Safety & Environment
   1.15 - Hostile environment
   1.16 - Language barriers

   - Campos por pregunta:
     * SCORE (0-10)
     * RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
     * TARGET DATE
     * ACTIONS TAKEN
     * REVISED SCORE

   - Features:
     * Part Number obligatorio
     * Cálculo automático de Average Risk Score
     * Criterios de evaluación en descripción de cada pregunta

   - Ruta: /work-instructions/:wiId/risk-assessments/new
